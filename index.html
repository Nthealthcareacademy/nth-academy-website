State approval & student complaints

North Texas Healthcare Academy is regulated by the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC), Career Schools and Colleges. TWC School Number: S63704. TWC Certificate of Approval: pending issuance; this notice will be updated when the certificate is issued. The school's Nurse Aide Training and Competency Evaluation Program (NATCEP) is approved by the Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC), NATCEP Program Code 7317 (approved May 28, 2026; expires May 28, 2028). If a student is dissatisfied with the school's response to a complaint, the student may file a formal complaint with the Texas Workforce Commission, Career Schools and Colleges, as well as with the school's accrediting body, if applicable. Additional information on the TWC complaint process is available on the Texas Workforce Commission's Career Schools and Colleges website.
