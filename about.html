Real training for real healthcare careers

North Texas Healthcare Academy prepares students across the DFW Metroplex for in-demand healthcare certifications, with hands-on instruction led by people who have done the work themselves.

We started North Texas Healthcare Academy on a simple belief: that a healthcare career should be within reach for anyone with the drive to care for others. Our programs are short, focused, and built around real clinical practice, so you leave with skills an employer can actually use, not just a certificate.

Our mission

Prepare students for meaningful healthcare careers

To equip our students with the practical skills, personal support, and confidence to succeed in healthcare, through hands-on training and a genuine commitment to each person who walks through our doors.

Our vision

Stronger careers, stronger communities

A North Texas where anyone ready to serve others can gain the credentials to build a lasting healthcare career, and where our graduates strengthen the clinics, hospitals, and families they serve.

What sets us apart

Instructors who've done the job

Every class is taught by someone who has worked the floor, not just a textbook. You'll train hands-on, ask real questions, and learn what the work is actually like.

Personal support

We keep our classes focused and our support personal, so you get real attention and no one gets lost along the way, from your first inquiry to exam day.

Rooted in the community

We're connected to the North Texas healthcare community, pairing classroom learning with supervised clinical practice in real care settings.

The programs we offer

Short, hands-on certifications built around real clinical practice, with more programs on the way.

  • Certified Nursing Assistant
  • Phlebotomy Technician
  • EKG Technician
  • Medical Assistant
  • Patient Care Technician
  • Medical Billing & Coding

North Texas Healthcare Academy is regulated by the Texas Workforce Commission, Career Schools and Colleges (School No. S63704), and our Nurse Aide Training and Competency Evaluation Program (NATCEP) is approved by the Texas Health and Human Services Commission. We serve students in Greenville and across Denton, Dallas, Fort Worth, and the wider DFW Metroplex.
